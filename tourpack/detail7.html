Destination Details

West Kelingking Beach

Kelingking Beach is the most iconic destination in Nusa Penida, famous for its unique cliff formation resembling a T-Rex. Overlooking crystal-clear turquoise waters and a pristine white sand beach below, this breathtaking viewpoint offers one of the most stunning panoramas in Bali. It’s a must-visit spot for capturing unforgettable photos and experiencing the raw beauty of the island.

West Broken Beach

Broken Beach is a natural wonder known for its circular cliff formation with a dramatic archway that allows ocean water to flow into a serene lagoon. The unique landscape creates a peaceful yet powerful ocean view, making it one of the most photogenic and memorable spots in Nusa Penida.

East Atuh Beach

Atuh Beach is a hidden paradise surrounded by towering cliffs and dramatic rock formations. With its soft white sand and calm, clear waters, this beach offers a perfect escape into nature. The scenic views from above and the peaceful atmosphere make it one of the most beautiful beaches on the island.

East Diamond Beach

Diamond Beach is one of Nusa Penida’s most stunning and famous destinations, known for its dramatic cliffs and crystal-clear waters. Accessible via a carved staircase along the cliff, this beach offers breathtaking views and a truly unique experience. The combination of white sand, turquoise ocean, and towering rock formations makes it a must-visit spot for every traveler.
