Top Hidden Beaches in Bali You Must Visit

Fun Bali Tour
•
March 29, 2026

Description

Bali is famous for its stunning beaches such as Kuta, Seminyak, and Sanur, which attract millions of tourists every year. However, beyond these popular destinations, the island also hides many beautiful secret beaches that remain less crowded and more peaceful. These hidden beaches are often located beneath dramatic cliffs or tucked away in remote areas, offering breathtaking ocean views and a more natural atmosphere. For travelers who want to escape the busy tourist spots and experience the untouched beauty of Bali, exploring hidden beaches can become one of the most memorable parts of their journey.

Why Hidden Beaches in Bali Are Special

Hidden beaches offer a different experience compared to Bali’s more famous beaches. Because they are often harder to access, these locations tend to be quieter and less commercialized.

Some reasons why hidden beaches are worth visiting include:

  • Peaceful atmosphere away from large crowds
  • Stunning natural scenery and dramatic cliffs
  • Crystal clear water and clean white sand
  • Perfect spots for photography and relaxation
  • A more authentic connection with nature

These beaches are perfect for travelers who prefer calm environments and want to discover a different side of Bali.

1. Nyang Nyang Beach

Nyang Nyang Beach is one of the most beautiful hidden beaches located in the southern part of Bali, near Uluwatu. This beach is known for its long stretch of white sand and untouched natural surroundings.

What makes Nyang Nyang Beach special:

  • Long and wide white sandy coastline
  • Very peaceful and rarely crowded
  • Amazing panoramic views from the cliff above
  • Perfect location for sunset photography

Because the beach requires a walk down a steep path from the cliff, fewer tourists visit this location, making it a perfect escape for travelers seeking tranquility.

2. Green Bowl Beach

Green Bowl Beach is another hidden gem located in the Ungasan area of South Bali. The beach is surrounded by limestone cliffs and offers a unique natural landscape.

Highlights of Green Bowl Beach include:

  • Clear turquoise water ideal for swimming
  • Natural caves around the beach area
  • Excellent waves for experienced surfers
  • Quiet and relaxing environment

To reach the beach, visitors must walk down hundreds of steps, but the stunning scenery at the bottom makes the journey worthwhile.

3. Bias Tugel Beach

Bias Tugel Beach, sometimes called “Little Padang Padang,” is located near the harbor town of Padang Bai in East Bali. This small beach is hidden behind rocky hills and offers calm and clear waters.

Things to enjoy at Bias Tugel Beach:

  • Soft white sand and calm waters
  • Ideal spot for swimming and snorkeling
  • Beautiful coastal scenery
  • A peaceful alternative to more crowded beaches

Because it is slightly hidden from the main road, the beach remains relatively quiet compared to other popular coastal destinations in Bali.

4. Gunung Payung Beach

Gunung Payung Beach is located in Kutuh Village and is considered one of the lesser-known beaches in South Bali. The beach offers spectacular views of the Indian Ocean and is surrounded by cliffs.

Reasons to visit Gunung Payung Beach:

  • Clean and beautiful coastline
  • Fewer tourists compared to other beaches
  • Scenic walking path leading down the cliff
  • Great location for relaxation and peaceful beach time

This beach is perfect for visitors who want to enjoy Bali’s coastal beauty without the crowds of more famous destinations.

Tips for Visiting Hidden Beaches in Bali

Because hidden beaches are often located in remote areas, it is important to prepare before visiting them.

Here are some helpful tips:

  • Wear comfortable shoes for walking or hiking
  • Bring enough drinking water
  • Visit during good weather conditions
  • Check the tide and wave conditions
  • Avoid leaving trash to protect the environment

Conclusion

While Bali’s popular beaches are well known around the world, the island’s hidden beaches offer a completely different experience. From dramatic cliffs to untouched white sand and crystal-clear waters, these secluded coastal spots reveal the natural beauty of Bali in a more peaceful setting. Exploring hidden beaches such as Nyang Nyang, Green Bowl, Bias Tugel, and Gunung Payung allows travelers to escape the crowds and discover some of the island’s most breathtaking landscapes. For anyone seeking adventure and tranquility, these hidden beaches are truly worth adding to your Bali travel itinerary.
